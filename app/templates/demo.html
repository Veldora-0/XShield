How this demonstration works

  1. Ingestion: The Portal sends input to XShield's shared analysis engine with endpoint {{ endpoint or "/portal/search" }}.
  2. Evaluation: Independent Rule (R001–R006) and ML (TF-IDF+LR) engines compute risk.
  3. Decision: If risk is High or Critical, the action engine blocks the payload before execution.
  4. Intelligence: The event is recorded in SQLite. Multiple probes trigger Phase 6 bursts and Phase 7 incident correlation cards in the Console.
