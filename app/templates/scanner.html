SCANNER GUIDANCE

Multi-Engine Analysis

Submitting text invokes the synchronous analyze_security_input pipeline, identical to production event observation:

1
Independent Heuristic Rules

R001–R006 check for unescaped markup, execution constructs, event handlers, and obfuscated URLs.

2
Character N-Gram ML Evaluation

Evaluated against 54,919 TF-IDF features to calculate a calibrated XSS probability.

3
Risk Engine & Fail-Closed Response

The continuous hybrid signal is scored (0–100) and assigned a defensive action (allow, flag, or block).
